Privacy Policy

PawLink: Cute Animal Connect · Effective July 30, 2026

1. Overview

PawLink ("PawLink", "we", "the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app and the third-party services it uses collect, why, and what choices you have. There is no account, login, or chat — almost everything below comes from the advertising service that keeps the app free.

2. Information we collect

Game progress — level unlocks, stars, high scores, Endless best stage, and your Music/SFX/hearts settings. Stored locally on your device only. We never see it, and it's deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising & device data — advertising ID, coarse location (from IP), device and app-interaction data used to serve and measure ads. Collected by Google AdMob, our ad provider (see Section 3).

We do not require an account and do not directly collect your name, email, or contact details through gameplay.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PawLink shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional "watch an ad" rewards) through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and other technical data to select, deliver, and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud. This processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps

If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, PawLink shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, so you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change that choice any time — see Section 5.

4. Children's privacy

PawLink is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age under your local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent choice: open Settings → Privacy Options any time (shown where required by your region).
  • Reset your ad ID / limit ad tracking: available in your device's own Settings app.
  • Delete your local data: uninstalling PawLink removes all locally stored progress and settings.
  • Reset progress in-app: Settings → Reset Progress clears your local save without uninstalling.

6. Data retention & security

Local save data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. We don't operate a backend server for PawLink, so we don't hold a copy of your save file or a profile of you — data handled for advertising is retained and secured according to Google's own policies linked in Section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Material changes will also be called out in the app's Privacy Policy screen for at least one version cycle.
